Our ICLR 2026 paper, [Composition of Pretrained Diffusion Models: A Logic-Based Calculus](https://openreview.net/forum?id=ADLiUSC7Qm), studies how to compose pretrained diffusion models using operators grounded in fuzzy logic.

The paper introduces conjunction, disjunction, and negation operators that generalize classical mixtures while improving mode coverage, stability under negation queries, and compositional behavior. It also studies Dombi operators for richer logical compositions and derives Feynman-Kac correctors to reduce sampling bias.
